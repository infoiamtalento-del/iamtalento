# Standardize Headings Across All Remaining Pages

## Scope
Apply the established homepage/assessment heading system to every remaining public-facing page, while leaving Homepage, Assessments, and Jobs unchanged.

Pages covered:
- Learning
- Podcasts and Webinars
- Networking, Meetups, and Hackathons
- Pricing, Candidate Pricing, and Employer Pricing
- For Employers pages
- About, Features, Contact, Blog, Changelog, Resources, and Documentation

Functional screens such as sign-in, profile builders, dashboards, assessment-taking screens, and profile records will keep their task-focused hierarchy. Their form labels, card titles, names, and data headings will not be forced into the marketing-section pattern.

## Changes
- Convert each true page or section headline into two deliberate rows on tablet and desktop.
- Keep the first headline row visibly longer and the shorter second row in the brand red.
- Use the shared heading scale and line height already established on Homepage and Assessments.
- Rewrite or rebalance copy where necessary so every introductory description is two deliberate rows, with the first row longer and the second shorter.
- Add concise descriptions to sections that currently have none.
- Preserve white heading treatments inside red/dark banners; use hierarchy and line breaks there without introducing low-contrast red text.
- Preserve existing content meaning, interactions, cards, forms, filters, media, and page structure.
- Keep mobile text naturally wrapping by applying deliberate breaks only from tablet widths upward.

## Quality Checks
- Review all affected routes at desktop and mobile widths.
- Confirm headings and descriptions do not overlap, overflow, or create awkward three-line wraps.
- Confirm interactive sections, tabs, filters, audio controls, pricing controls, and links still work.
- Confirm Homepage, Assessments, and Jobs are unchanged.
- Resolve any preview build errors before completion.
